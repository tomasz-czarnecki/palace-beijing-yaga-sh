# Research

Evidence base for the 3D reconstruction of the Old Summer Palace (圆明园 Yuanmingyuan), target state just before October 1860.
Rule: nothing goes into the model without a source.

## Files

- `sources.md`: catalogue of every source (S01, S02, ...).
- `questions.md`: open questions and conflicts between sources. Q1 is the target state.
- `wishlist.md`: what would replace the biggest guesses, where it is held, how to ask.
- `buildings.csv`: one row per compound (`YM..`, `CC..`, `XY..`, `QC..`) and one per hall or structure inside it. Generated by `scripts/build_r1.py`; change its inputs, not the rows.
- `layers/`: working GeoJSON in WGS84, the hand-off to Round 2: `walls_1860` (walls as lines, gates as points), `water_1860`, `islands_1860`, `hills_1860`, `bridges_1860`, `scenes_1860`, `footprints_1860`, `fields_1860` (the labelled 稻田 rice fields), `osm_today` (ODbL).
- `plan/`: everything read off the base plan S01, in plan pixels: `blocks.csv` (detected hall rectangles), `regions.json`, `regions_classified.csv`, `walls.json`, `gates.json`, `slips.json`, the manual inputs of `scripts/trace_plan.py`; `readings/<area>/` (five readers: `compounds.csv`, `named.csv`, `blocks_fix.csv`, `features.csv`, `notes.md`); `inventory_texts.csv` (rows from the texts, with the quotes in `rel_position`); `osm_points.csv`, `hills_texts.csv`, `photos_raw.csv`, `new_ids.csv`, `coverage.json`.
- `georef/`: `princeton-mosaic.json` (how the three scrolls are stitched), `princeton-1920s.csv` (control points, with datum and use) and `princeton-1920s.json` (transform, RMS, leave-one-out, per-point residuals, per-area verdict).
- `typology/`: `halls.md`, `params.json` (defaults by hall class, each with a source and a confidence), `rules_out.json`, `scale_check.json`.
- `terrain/README.md`: what the DEM can and cannot tell.
- `photos/`: the picture catalog, see `photos/README.md`.
- `raw/`: downloaded originals (plans, DEM, OSM answer, research-only pictures). Local only, never committed.
- `overlays/`: `princeton-1920s.jpg` + `.json` (bounds for a Leaflet imageOverlay). Local only.
- `buildings/`: one page per row of `buildings.csv` and per gate, `buildings/README.md` the list. Generated by `scripts/building_pages.py` except each page's `## Notes`.

Everything from `publish_layers.py` down needs only `buildings.csv`, `layers/` and `typology/params.json`, and runs in seconds. After any change to those, rerun those five in order.

`scripts/georef.py` exposes `plan_to_wgs84(xs, ys)` and `wgs84_to_plan(lons, lats)`.

## Conventions

**Source IDs.** Each source in `sources.md` has an ID; every row cites one or more, separated by `;` (`S03;S12`).

**Confidence** (`buildings.csv`, GeoJSON), as in `CLAUDE.md`: A = shown on a source made while the gardens stood, or measured on remains unchanged. B = a later source, or a written description only. C = inferred, every guess. How it is applied here:
- `confidence` = the row: does this thing, in this form, belong to the state of 1860? Album scenes are A unless S41 says later plans differ (then B). A hall named from the texts is B. A block drawn on S01 with no name is B: a building stood here, name unknown. An identification marked `guess` is C.
- `pos_confidence` = the position. **S01 counts as B, not A, until Q2 is settled:** it may be a 1920s tracing. `guess` readings and OSM points are C.
- `read` = how the plan reader identified it. `sure` (label legible beside it, or Malone's number with matching buildings), `likely`, `guess`, `not_found`.

**plan_x, plan_y** always mean pixels on `raw/plans/princeton_mosaic.jpg`, 12955 × 9049, origin top-left. The plan is turned about 7.2° from true north. Ground scale 4.77 px/m, locally 3.7–5.55: never size a building from it.

**Blank cells** mean unknown or not read.

**Pinyin.** Names from the texts carry the inventory's pinyin. Names first read on the plan are romanised by `pypinyin` and not checked by a person; a name with an unread `?` has none.

## Layers

Every feature carries `id`, names where known, `sources`, `confidence`, `note`.
- `footprints_1860`: one rectangle per box, as drawn on the plan and warped. **Use its centre with `orientation_deg`, `frontage_m`, `depth_m`, `hall_class`,** not its outline: halls are drawn smaller than the ground scale, so the true size is larger than the outline. `building_id` links to the row. Also `block`, `source_kind`, `ncols`, `nrows`.
- `scenes_1860`: one polygon per compound, drawn by the readers round its island or court. `read`, `malone_no`, `area_m2`. Not a boundary known from a source.
- `water_1860`: `kind` lake, pond or canal; holes are islands. `islands_1860`: land enclosed by the water layer closed by 45 px (about 9 m), plus Malone's nine islands from their scene polygons.
- `hills_1860`: `height_est_m`, `height_method`; all C.
- `bridges_1860`: lines between the two ends. The drawn width is not recorded.
- `walls_1860`: `kind` outer_wall, separating_wall (lines) and gate (points). Read by eye, 10–20 px off the ink, Qichun 20–40 px.

## The boxes and the ground (Round 2)

Constants with their sources stand at the top of `scripts/boxes1860.py` and `scripts/ground1860.py`, levels in `scripts/scene1860.py`.

- **`halls_1860`:** one feature per box, `basis` says what it stands on: `plan` (a block drawn on S01), `stated` (a size in a source), `xiyang`, `layout`, `default`, `gate` (these four `assumed: true`, C). Size comes down from the hall scale towards the drawn size where a neighbour or water needs it (`fit`), and may move a little (`moved_m`). Q34.
- **Heights and roofs:** `typology/params.json` by `hall_class`; a stated hall in `by_name` wins. A hall deeper than 1.5 x its class's depth takes parallel rolls (`rolls`).
- **Xiyang Lou:** massing from `params.json` `xiyang_lou`, placed by hand in `xiyang_lou()` from the rows' positions; not from the footprints, which the plan draws schematically. Q35.
- **No box:** rows with no position, labelled plots, the 寿山 ridge, water, docks, sluices (reasons in `boxes1860.py`).
- **Ground:** flat base land 45 m and one water level 43.5 m (EGM2008), from `terrain/README.md`; islands +0.8 m, hills as mounds from `hills_1860`, 线法山 from `params.json`. No DEM cell is used as a height: it holds today's trees and fill. Land is cut exactly to `water_1860`, with a bank down to the water. Q34.
- **Walls:** `walls_1860` with height 2.8 m and thickness 0.8 m (`params.json`). A wall stops at a hall standing in its line (53 boxes), and opens 6 m at a gate with no hall. A gate point within 14 m of a wall line counts as in it.

## Public and private

- **Public, with credit** ("Image courtesy of the Princeton University Art Museum"): geometry traced from S01, that is, every `*_1860` layer and the positions, in `site/reconstruction/data/` through `scripts/publish_layers.py`, which drops the readers' working fields. The museum's data treats S01 as not under copyright, and its pages say so (S01 in `sources.md`, Q6).
- **Local for now:** the plan image and the overlay. The museum's terms would allow showing them, but they carry a non-commercial clause and Tomasz has not decided (Q6). So `research/raw/` and `research/overlays/` stay out of git and off the site; `tools/serve_local.py` serves the overlay on localhost under `/_local/`, as Küstrin does. It marks its answers with `X-Palace-Local`, so a page on a plain static server (the preview on :8790, a host) never asks for `/_local/`. `?public=1` shows what a visitor sees.
- **Building pages:** `data/buildings/<ID>.json` holds names, garden, dates, sources, confidence, the 日下旧闻考 quote where `rel_position` has one (S05, public domain), the note as facts with citations, the box, and pictures as link cards. Only `raw/buildings/<ID>.json` has the plan readers' notes, plan pixels and any other quote.
- **Pictures:** `photos/images/` holds free images only; research-only ones stay in `raw/photos/`. BnF album leaves need the credit "Source gallica.bnf.fr / BnF".
- **`research/plan/inventory_texts.csv`** quotes 日下旧闻考 (public domain) and states facts from research-only articles; it is not published as a file. Facts go to the site with a citation only.
- OSM data needs ODbL attribution. Esri tiles are never saved in the repo.
