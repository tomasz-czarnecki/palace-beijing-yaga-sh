# ym_west notes (S01, north and west of Yuanming Yuan)

Readings by eye from about 13 base tiles and label zooms. Faint labels were checked with contrast crops. Malone p. numbers are from `malone66.txt`.

## Reading problems
- Many labels are in dark ink and clear. A second layer is very faint (日天琳宇, 鸿慈永祜, the labels at 多稼轩 / 观稼轩, the two columns at (3180,2330), the line above the oblong at (2730,1885)). Where I could not read them they carry `?`.
- Detection is fragmented on complex halls (慎修思永, 水云居, 日天琳宇 long gallery); see `blocks_fix.csv`. Bay counts in `blocks.csv` are often short of the drawing.
- Pencil additions, not buildings: the outline with "20" and "11" at (3630-3900,2430-2510), the sketch at (1120,5070), a stain near (1700,2800).
- No pasted slip seen in this area.

## Identifications worth checking (they use the text, not a label)
- 安佑宫 approach: the small squares and bar at (908-1050,2033-2167) were read as the four pillars and pailou from Malone p.84-85, and the U-moat bars at y 1692 as his five bridges. Text j81:20a says 石华表 左右各一 (two); plan and Malone show four.
- 多稼轩 = B0411: seven bays, and the faint label ends in 轩 with a middle char like 稼. This puts the 别垣 yard (2930-3130,2340-2470) under YM20, not under Malone 21 next to it.
- 映水兰香 itself (five bays facing west) NOT securely found. Nearest candidates: B0374 is labelled 丰乐轩 in dark ink, B0431 reads as 观稼轩. YM21 is placed on the islet beside Malone's 21 as a stopgap; 印月池 and 知耕织 sit far north of it (inside YM22). Treat YM21 as weak.
- 魁星楼 = ring at (632,2283), from S47 position and Malone's "round tower". No label.

## Plan against text
- 涵虚朗鉴 is written clearly under B0355 at the 花神庙 group (2593,1993). The same name is Forty Scene YM36 on Fuhai. The brief already says Malone's 30 and 36 may be swapped, so compare with the ym_east reading. I did NOT move YM36.
- 澹泊宁静: label's third char looks like 清, not 宁.
- 藻园 labels differ from j81:8b: 自远轩, ?琴??, ?藻楼, ??琴峡 are not in the text, and the hall in the position of 旷然堂 reads ?家芳. 贮清书屋, 镜澜榭, 怀新馆, 湛碧轩 agree.
- 月地云居: plan names 都罡殿 (square front hall) and 三世佛殿 (next hall); text only 前殿 / 殿. Malone lists a drum and bell tower and two tablet pavilions; plan has two pairs of octagons, (925,2715)/(1050,2715) and (918,3240)/(1060,3238), unassigned between those uses.
- 日天琳宇: rows of 8 and 5 bays against 7 in text for each of the four towers. Mapping of 西/中 前楼 follows the 日天琳宇 and 佛楼 labels, but counts disagree.
- 文源阁: I count 5 bays; text 六楹.
- Rixia halls not visible as distinct buildings: much of YM20 (溪山不尽, 兰溪隐玉, 寸碧, 引胜, 稻香亭), 云香清胜, 香雪廊, 芰荷深处, and most of YM14's named pavilions and of YM26's list. They may be unnamed blocks; no row written.
- Gates: text's west 随墙门 against plan's 西北门 (guess); text's 北楼门 against plan's 大北门 (likely).

## Dating evidence
- Terminus post quem: 文源阁 (Malone p.89-90: 1774 monument) is drawn.
- Names on the plan that the Rixia-based inventory lacks: 知?堂, 水云居, 墨?亭 at 濂溪乐处; 自远轩 etc. at 藻园; 极乐世界, 都罡殿, 三世佛殿. 恒春堂 and its stage are in the inventory only through S43, not Rixia. So the drawing is later than the Rixia state in these places, or uses other plaques. I have no dated source for any of these halls, so this does not fix a reign.
- No dated Daoguang or Xianfeng hall could be confirmed in this area, and I found no known-1860 hall definitely missing. **Which reign: not determined here; after 1774, and probably after the 1780s text.**

## Schematic or uncertain drawing
- 日天琳宇 long gallery, 田字 hall grid (cell count rough), 武陵春色 north yard. Fully drawn bay by bay elsewhere.

## Regions
- Closed lobed outlines in the northern strip (R0933 and neighbours) have no label, wash or hatching; classes left unchanged. Double-lined ovals are drawn like the labelled 稻田, hence the guesses in `features.csv` (R0273, R0284).
- Label 稻田 at (3885,2575) falls on no region at that point; the field oval there needs a check, along with R0120 and R0202.
