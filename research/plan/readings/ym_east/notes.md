# ym_east reader notes (S01, x 4300-8660, y 100-5800)

## Reading
- **Malone's 30 and 36 are swapped on the plan.** 36 stands at (5590,3400) by 澄虚榭 (YM30); 30 stands at (7890,1960) off 雷峰夕照 (YM36). Rows follow the labels.
- Usual habit of the labels: written on the court or ground just south of the hall they name, or on the water side of a west- or east-facing hall. Where a label sits between two halls I took the one to its north, unless the text says otherwise (前垂天贶).
- Bays: the plan and the text agree where both are clear: 廓然大公 7, 蓬岛瑶台 7, 别有洞天 5, 麴院风荷 5, 观澜堂 5x3, 云锦墅 5, 惠如春 2. They differ at: 澄虚榭 (text 3, plan 5 cells), 平湖秋月 (text 3, plan 4x4), 坐石临流 (text 3, plan one room), 鱼跃鸢飞 (text 5, plan 6x5).
- Names on the plan differ from the text: 贻兰亭 (庭), 福元门 (园), 大北门 (北楼门), 宫门 (山门), 春和镇 (宁和镇), 雷峰照 (雷峰夕照, 夕 not visible), 北岛 labelled 值房.

## Dating evidence
S111 = 北平旧梦 blog quoting 《圆明园百景图志》, already in the typology notes: research-only, B at best.
- **观澜堂 is drawn and labelled, as a 5x3 hall.** S111: 三卷五间, built about 1817 replacing the south court of 接秀山房 (S51 via the inventory notes: replaced 观鱼跃). So this part of the drawing is later than about 1817.
- **No 接秀山房 label, and none for 琴趣轩, 寻云楼, 澄练楼, 怡然书屋, 安隐幢, 揽翠亭.** S111: those names no longer appear in Daoguang and Xianfeng records. The plan agrees with the later state.
- 云锦墅 as a five-bay west-facing hall, and 值房 north of 贻兰亭, match S111's Daoguang-Xianfeng descriptions.
- **乐道堂**, west 所 of 洞天深处: from memory this is the hall name of Prince Gong 奕訢, given under Daoguang. Not verified, no source at hand. If confirmed, it points to the 1840s or later. Worth a source check.
- Nothing here gives an upper limit. No hall in this box is known to be Xianfeng-era. 舍卫城, 同乐园 and 方壶胜境 match the Qianlong text.
- **Reign shown: post-1817, most likely Daoguang-Xianfeng.** Good for the 1860 target in this area.
- Known in 1860 and missing: none that I can prove. 万景天全 (Xianfeng plaque, S111: exact place unknown) has no label; 翠云幄 likewise, guess row at B0300.
