# YM02-01 勤政殿 Qinzheng Dian: the emperor's office hall of 勤政亲贤, a small detailed model (Round 19)

勤政殿, the main hall of 勤政亲贤 Qinzheng Qinxian, "Diligent in Government and Friendly with Officials", east of the audience court of 正大光明: here the emperor read memorials and received officials day by day when the court was in the garden (日於此披省章奏召對臣工, S05 j80:18a). Drawn by the generator of 正大光明 (`models/YM01-zhengda-guangming/zhengdaguangming.py`), on its own frame (the box F0008) so that its panel frames it; its layout read on S01 in that model's `plan_readings.csv` (`qz_*`).

## Evidence

- **The plan (A):** 勤政殿地盘图样 (mhymy ysltd 7310, free part) and the list card ysltd/11009 (S310): five bays facing south with a three-bay porch 抱厦 front and back; S05 j80:11b 正大光明殿東為勤政親賢正殿五楹 (A). S211 gives three bays (buildings.csv notes), against both.
- **The plan on the ground (B):** S01 (Round 19 reading on a 4x crop): the whole width x 3627.5-3734 (about 22 m), the middle three cells running on south and north (the porches), a walled court before it with a small gate in its south wall.
- **The plaques (A):** 額曰勤政親賢; 後楹額曰為君難 (S05 j80:18a).
- **A change of the Daoguang reign (B):** in 丁酉, most likely 1837, the east study got a front porch, a south door, north windows with a kang and large glass panes (S211); inside, not drawn.
- **The hall classes (C):** every height, the roofs (rolled 歇山, grey), the porches' roofs joined to the hall's slopes, the court's walls and gate.

## What the model draws

The hall of five bays (22 m on S01) with its front and rear porches of three bays, each on the hall's platform, rolled 歇山 roofs in grey, a flight before each porch; the plaques; the court before it, paved, walled in white with a gate in the south wall.
