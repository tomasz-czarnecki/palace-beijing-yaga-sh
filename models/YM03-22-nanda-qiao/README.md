# YM03-22 南大桥 Nan Da Qiao: a bridge of 九洲清晏, a detailed model (Round 18)

One of the three named bridges of the island 九洲清晏 YM03, drawn by the axis model's generator (`models/YM03-jiuzhou-qingyan/jiuzhouqingyan.py`) as a model of its own, so that its panel's small view frames it. Bridge BR001 of Round 9 (`research/layers/bridges_1860`), from the island's south-west corner to the shore of 正大光明 (S01, label sure); the view leaves out Round 10's plain deck for it (`site/reconstruction/data/models.json` `bridges`).

## Evidence

- **Position and length:** S01, the strip Round 9 traced (B); the deck's levels from Round 10 (`site/reconstruction/generated/structures.json`), so that walking and the ways stay on it.
- **Form, today:** P0367: today's bridge rebuilt on dressed-stone abutments with a timber superstructure and railings (B for the abutments; the timber is modern).
- **Form, period:** the 1744 leaf P0003: a flat timber bridge with red-brown railings on the island's west side (A for 1744).
- **C:** the span's timber, the posts under the deck where it stands over water (every 3 m), the railings' rhythm (a post every 1.6 m, two rails), the abutments' size (1.6 m along the bridge, 0.5 m beyond the deck each side).

## Joints

Its two ends are joints with the ground of Round 10 (`tools/check_models.py`); the posts stand on the bed. No box of its own: the bridge is clicked through its row's page.
