# YM01 正大光明 Zhengda Guangming: the formal entrance of the garden, a detailed model (Round 19)

The ninth detailed model and the third of a Chinese compound: the official approach of the Yuanming Yuan from the terrace before 大宫门 Da Gongmen, the Great Palace Gate, through the inner court and over the moon-shaped canal to the second gate 出入贤良门 Churu Xianliang Men, and across the flagged court to the audience hall 正大光明殿 Zhengda Guangming Dian, "Upright and Bright", with the rock peaks of 寿山 behind it. The state is the 1850s: the hall as built in 1725 and dressed in the later eighteenth century (the terrace 月台 with its four censers, 1775-1793; red columns), the bridge levelled flat in 1839 (S211), the courts as the witnesses of October 1860 saw them. French troops reached the gate on 6 October 1860 and the gardens were looted in the days to 8 October; during the burning of 18-19 October ordered by Elgin, British troops quartered at the hall set it alight last (S02 pp.179-188; M'Ghee p.288, E1028). The site of the hall is a lawn today with the rock hill behind it (P0363); the dig at 大宫门 found the gate hall, its wing gates, the 金水桥 and the channel (E2120).

Generator: `zhengdaguangming.py` (`uv run python models/YM01-zhengda-guangming/zhengdaguangming.py`), on `scripts/hall_parts.py` (the hall kit in Python, Round 17) and `scripts/model_site.py`. Hand-edited sheet: `plan_readings.csv` (every line read on S01, in plan pixels). Writes `model.glb`, `params.json`, `keypoints.json`, `joints.json`, `summary.json`, `report.json`, and two small models of their own beside this folder: 勤政殿 `models/YM02-01-qinzheng-dian/` and the spirit wall `models/YM41-12-da-yingbi/`.

## Match check

The only period picture of the scene is the album leaf of 1744 (P0001), a painting, not a perspective, and of the state before the terrace (1775-1793) and before the bridge was levelled (1839): 16 points read on 3.5x and 5x crops of the 1920 px copy (`research/scenes/YM01-zhengda-guangming/plate_keypoints.csv`): the eave tips, ridge ends and front corner columns' feet of the hall and of 出入贤良门, and the outer eave tips and corner columns of the two wings. A free pinhole camera (`tools/plate_overlay.py fit --sheets research/scenes/YM01-zhengda-guangming`): **3.6 m rms at the object (median 3.0 m, max 6.2 m) on 16 points** (the numbers of the final run in the round's entry, `docs/rounds/README.md`). The overlay (`screenshots/r19/overlay_P0001_YM01_all_*`) lays the gate, its wings and the hall on the leaf's; the leaf paints the gate's east end and the hall's east end further east than the model (15-24 px) and every column taller (the feet 10-16 px lower), as it paints the halls 1.5-1.9 times too tall elsewhere (Q198, Q263). The 1745 woodcut P0100 and its re-cut P0232 repeat the leaf's layout; the gouache P0102 recolours it (Q128); no ruin photograph shows a hall: none was fitted.
