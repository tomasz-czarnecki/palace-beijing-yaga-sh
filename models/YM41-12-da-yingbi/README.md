# YM41-12 大影壁 Da Yingbi: the spirit wall south of 大宫门, a small detailed model (Round 19)

The spirit wall that closed the road on the axis south of 大宫门, between the two halves of the Front Lake (S05 j80:7b; Malone, S02 p.69). It survived 1860 and was torn down for its materials after Malone measured it. Drawn by the generator of 正大光明 (`models/YM01-zhengda-guangming/zhengdaguangming.py`), on that model's axis.

## Evidence

- **Size (B):** the 正大光明 survey report: 东西长 41.6 m (S102, E1017); Malone, who measured it before it was torn down: 137 ft long, over 5 ft thick, about 15 ft high, 'ornamented' (S02 p.69, E2114).
- **Place (B):** 自宫门月台南沿至影壁 204.8 m (S102, E1017), on the axis; beyond the plan S01, which ends about 140 m south of the gate, so the bearing is the model's axis (C).
- **Form (C):** a stone base, the faces framed with a middle panel, a grey tiled hip coping: the screen wall's typology (`HallKit.screen`); Malone gives no more than 'ornamented'.
- **Not drawn:** the two halves of the Front Lake 前湖 (扇面湖) either side of the road (S05 j80:7b-8a; Malone's 'Fan Lakes'): they lie beyond S01 and Round 10 has no water there (wishlist).
