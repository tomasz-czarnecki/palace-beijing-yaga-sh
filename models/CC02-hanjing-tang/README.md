# CC02 含经堂 Hanjingtang: the palace compound of 长春园, a detailed model (Round 17)

The first detailed model of a Chinese compound: the walled compound on the central island of 长春园 Changchun Yuan and its forecourt, as they stood before October 1860, when French and British troops looted the gardens and British troops burned them on Elgin's order (S02 pp.179-188). No picture of any hall standing survives; the compound is known from the plan S01, the Yangshi Lei plans of 长春园淳化轩 (as S940 prints them), the Qing texts, the paper S940 that reads the dig of 2001-2003, and the excavated ruin as displayed today (the free photographs of 2007 and 2013).

Generator: `hanjingtang.py` (`uv run python models/CC02-hanjing-tang/hanjingtang.py`), on `scripts/hall_parts.py` (the hall kit of Round 11 in Python) and `scripts/model_site.py`. Hand-edited sheet: `plan_readings.csv` (every line read on S01, in plan pixels). Writes `model.glb`, `params.json`, `keypoints.json`, `joints.json`, `summary.json`, `report.json`.

## What the model draws, and on what

- **The forecourt** at the ground of Round 10: walls on three sides (S01), the three glazed archways in them at their boxes (S05; the form the class's, C), the crossing paths (S940), no trees (four lawns, S940).
- **The terrace** the compound stands on: 2.1 m above the forecourt (measured on P0403, P0505, P0815 by a sweep of the model, d, C), its curved edge from S01, the gate's projection 13.5 m wide with a three-part flight (the ruin, not S01's 24 m: Q244), a flight on each curve (S01's hatched blocks, C). Its floor is one paved deck (`joints.json` `decks`): the ways of Round 9 lie on it; the planting keeps off it and off the forecourt (`clear`).
- **The walls:** the outer wall (S01), the walls of the 含经堂 court, the cross walls (S01, read loosely, C); heights C.
- **The gate 宫门** at its box (S05, S01), five bays, the kit's gate with a bracket band (C).
- **The 含经堂 court:** the screen (S01, S940; its 0.4 m base from P0402), the two side halls (the west at its box F0317, the east mirrored, S940), the ⊐ ranges 梵香楼 and 霞翥楼 of two storeys (S01's plan, S05's names, C).
- **含经堂:** seven bays and a porch all round (S05, S940; S01's nine cells), double eaves 歇山 with a ridge (S940, S361), yellow glazed (glazed S361, the colour C, Q143), on a platform of 1.5 m with a front terrace of 0.8 m and a 4.3 m flight (P0402, d).
- **The galleries 墨刻游廊** east and west of the rockery court, the wall on the outer side with the dark slabs of the 淳化阁帖 (S05, S940 p.361); the rockery between 含经堂 and 淳化轩 (S01, S940; placement C).
- **淳化轩:** seven by three bays with porches (S940), rolled 歇山 glazed (S940, colour C), its terrace 17 x 5.5 m with two flights 2.5-3 m in from its ends (S01, P0404).
- **蕴真斋:** seven bays, porches front and back (S940; S01's cross), the front arm a terrace, rolled grey 歇山 (C).
- **The side courts:** every box inside the wall drawn by the kit from its own entry (class, `fill`), 三友轩 at its measured size (S940), 渊映斋 with five bays and the platform of P0410, the dressing room's platform of P0410, the stage as a two-storey pavilion (C); the north gate and its flight down to the hill (S940 names it; form C).
