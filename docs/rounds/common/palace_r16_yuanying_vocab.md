# 远瀛观 Yuanyingguan (XY09) and 方外观 Fangwaiguan (XY06): key-point vocabulary for the readers (Round 16)

Both face south. On every picture taken from the south, **west is the left**.

**远瀛观 XY09** (plate 14 = P0054, seen from the south, high, over 大水法's niche): a hall on a raised terrace behind (north of) 大水法. Its plan is an inverted 凹: two **wings** (side pavilions) come forward at the ends; between them the **centre block** stands back, with the **clock gate** on the axis: the door between two **carved columns** on pedestals (lions before them on the plate), an entablature block over each column, and above the cornice an attic with a round **clock wheel** under an arched moulding and a crest. The centre block has one window each side of the columns. Its roof is in tiers: a lower roof of fish-scale tiles either side of the clock gate, the main hipped roof above, a small top roof and a crowning ornament. Each wing has one big window in its front, a cornice, a low roof, a balustrade round a square **lantern** with a round clock face on each side, a small pyramid roof and a finial. Before the hall, the paved **forecourt** of the terrace with trees and balustrades; the front balustrade with urns on its posts; in the plate's foreground the curved balustrades of 大水法's stairs (not 远瀛观: do not read).
The ruin photographs (1873 to today) show the clock gate front: the two carved columns with their capitals and entablature blocks, the door's arched carved hood, the attic with the wheel (whole in 1873-76, P0183, P0190), the arched windows either side (P0179, P0772), brick walls behind. Today four columns stand free (the two carved ones and the jambs of the side arches) with a lintel (P0757, P0341).

**方外观 XY06** (plate 8 = P0048, seen from the south on the axis): two storeys, three bays. The **podium** (ground storey) is rusticated; in its middle a projecting **porch** with the door (a carved hood over it) and round steps before it; either side of the porch an **oval window** in a carved frame; at the ends panels of stacked blocks. The **upper storey** stands on the podium behind a balustrade (a balcony over the porch in the middle): three tall windows with scrolled pediments, carved pilaster strips at the corners, a cornice with rosettes. A **double-eaved roof**: a lower roof, then the upper roof with a ridge, a crest cartouche in the middle of the ridge and scrolled ornaments at its ends. Either end, a **curved stair** with scroll balustrades sweeps from the ground in front of the corner outward and back up to a landing at the upper storey's end (P0773, 1873, shows the west one from the south-west, the roof still on). In the plate's foreground, scrolled balustrades flank the axial walk (not the building: do not read).

## Points (name: definition)

### 远瀛观 XY09
Wings (W = the left wing on a south view, E = the right)
- `wing_W_foot_SW`, `wing_W_foot_SE`, `wing_E_foot_SW`, `wing_E_foot_SE`: the front (south) corners of the wing's front face at its foot (where the wall meets the base or the terrace floor).
- `wing_W_cornice_SW`, `wing_W_cornice_SE`, `wing_E_cornice_SW`, `wing_E_cornice_SE`: the same corners at the top of the wing's main cornice.
- `wing_W_win_head`, `wing_E_win_head`, `wing_W_win_sill`, `wing_E_win_sill`: the top and the bottom of the opening of the big window in the wing's front (inside the carved frame), at mid-width.
- `lantern_W_top`, `lantern_E_top`: the top of the lantern's finial. `lantern_W_eave_L`, `lantern_W_eave_R` (and `_E_`): the lantern roof's front eave corners, left and right as seen. `lantern_W_clock`, `lantern_E_clock`: the centre of the clock face on the lantern's front. `lantern_W_rail_L`, `lantern_W_rail_R` (and `_E_`): the top of the balustrade round the lantern's foot at its front corners, left and right as seen.

Centre block and clock gate
- `ctr_cornice_L`, `ctr_cornice_R`: the top of the centre block's main cornice at its outer front corners, left and right as seen.
- `ctr_foot_L`, `ctr_foot_R`: the centre block's front corners at the foot.
- `col_W_cap`, `col_E_cap`: the top of the carved column's capital (under the entablature block), on the column's front. `col_W_base`, `col_E_base`: the foot of the column's shaft (the top of its pedestal), front. `col_W_ped_foot`, `col_E_ped_foot`: the foot of the pedestal, front middle.
- `ent_W_top`, `ent_E_top`: the top front middle of the entablature block over each column (its cornice's top edge).
- `door_head`, `door_sill`: the top (crown of the arch) and the threshold of the central door's opening, inside its frame, on the axis.
- `ctrwin_W_head`, `ctrwin_E_head`: the top (crown) of the opening of the centre block's window either side of the columns. `ctrwin_W_foot_L`, `ctrwin_W_foot_R` (and `_E_`): the bottom corners of that opening (on a ruin, where its jambs meet the sill or the ground as they stand).
- `clock_C`: the centre of the clock wheel in the attic. `clock_arch_top`: the top of the arched moulding round the wheel. `clock_crest_top`: the top of the crest over it.
- `attic_scroll_L`, `attic_scroll_R`: the outermost tip of the big scroll at the attic's foot, left and right (on P0183 the scrolls ending over the entablature blocks).

Roofs (plate only)
- `scale_eave_L`, `scale_eave_R`: the outer front tip of the lower fish-scale roof either side of the clock gate.
- `roof_C_eave_L`, `roof_C_eave_R`: the front corner tips of the main hipped roof over the centre.
- `roof_C_apex`: the top of the main hipped roof on the axis (where the small top roof begins).
- `crown_top`: the top of the crowning ornament.

Terrace (plate only)
- `terr_rail_C`: the top of the front balustrade's rail on the axis.
- `terr_rail_L`, `terr_rail_R`: the top of the front balustrade at its outer ends, where it turns back (left and right as seen).
- `hall_step_C`: the front edge of the lowest step before the hall's centre, on the axis.

### 方外观 XY06
Podium (ground storey)
- `pod_foot_SW`, `pod_foot_SE`: the podium's front outer corners at the foot.
- `pod_top_SW`, `pod_top_SE`: the same corners at the top of the podium's cornice (the upper floor's level).
- `porch_foot_L`, `porch_foot_R`: the porch's front outer corners at the foot (behind the round steps), left and right as seen.
- `porch_top_L`, `porch_top_R`: the porch's front outer corners at the top of its cornice (under the balcony's balustrade).
- `door_head`, `door_sill`: the top and the threshold of the door's opening, inside its frame, at mid-width.
- `oval_W_top`, `oval_W_foot`, `oval_E_top`, `oval_E_foot`: the top and the bottom of each oval window's opening, inside its frame.
- `step_foot_C`: the front edge of the lowest round step before the door, on the axis.

Upper storey and roof
- `up_cornice_SW`, `up_cornice_SE`: the top of the upper storey's cornice at its front outer corners (under the roof).
- `upwin_C_head`, `upwin_C_sill`, `upwin_W_head`, `upwin_E_head`: the top and the bottom of the opening of the middle upper window, and the top of the side ones (inside the frame, at mid-width).
- `bal_rail_C`: the top of the balcony's rail on the axis. `bal_rail_L`, `bal_rail_R`: the top of the balustrade at its outer ends on the front (left and right as seen).
- `eave_lo_SW`, `eave_lo_SE`: the lower roof's front corner tips. `eave_up_SW`, `eave_up_SE`: the upper roof's front corner tips.
- `ridge_W`, `ridge_E`: the ends of the ridge (on the ridge line, under the end ornaments). `crest_top_C`: the top of the crest cartouche on the middle of the ridge.

Stairs
- `stair_W_foot`, `stair_E_foot`: the middle of the front edge of the lowest step.
- `stair_W_top`, `stair_E_top`: the top of the stair's outer balustrade where it reaches the upper landing (its highest end).
- `stair_W_out`, `stair_E_out`: the outermost point of the stair's outer balustrade top as seen (its extreme left for the W stair, right for the E).
- `stair_W_rail_foot`, `stair_E_rail_foot`: the top of the outer balustrade at its lowest end (the scroll at the stair's foot).
- `end_W_win_head`: the top of the opening in the upper storey's west end face (the door or window on the landing, P0773). `up_cornice_NW`: the top of the upper cornice at the north-west corner, if a picture shows it.

## How to read
- Pixel x, y in the copy `research/photos/images/<photo_id>.jpg` (or `research/raw/photos/<photo_id>.jpg` for a research-only picture) at its native size (plates 2400 px wide). Origin top left.
- `sure`: 1 sharp and unambiguous; 2 good; 3 rough (hidden by a tree, worn, a ruin's edge). A point you cannot find: leave it out. A reading that is not quite the defined point: write it with `use` = `skip: <reason>`.
- Check every reading: draw a small cross at it on a fresh crop (Python PIL, `/opt/data/workspace/000_Dev/palace-beijing-yaga-sh/.venv/bin/python`), look at the crop, correct, and say in the note what you saw.
- Ruin photographs (1873-today): read only what still stands as built. Never guess a vanished part. Fallen and re-set stones: skip unless they plainly stand in place.
- On a 大水法 view, the niche and piers in front are another building (XY10): never read them as 远瀛观.
