# palace_r7 common brief for every subagent

Project: `/opt/data/workspace/000_Dev/palace-beijing-yaga-sh`, a reconstruction of the Old Summer Palace 圆明园 as it stood before October 1860. Rulebook: its `CLAUDE.md` (read "Research rules", "Copyright", "Working rules"). Pitfalls: `docs/METHOD.md`, sections "Pitfalls and fixes" and "What blocked". **This round does for every scene and compound outside the Xiyang Lou what Round 6 did for the Xiyang Lou:** a dossier per scene, ready for detailed models. The model to copy is `research/xiyanglou/XY08-haiyan-tang/README.md` (read its head). Target state: just before October 1860; the 1744 album counts wherever nothing later contradicts it, and later changes (Jiaqing, Daoguang, Xianfeng) must be stated.

**The brief can be wrong.** Round 6's brief made three claims that did not hold. If a name, id or fact given to you is wrong, say so in a `question` row and work from what the source says.

## Hard rules
- **Nothing inside the repo.** Read only. No git commands. The coordinator alone writes repo files.
- **Write as you go,** so a crash keeps partial work:
  - free-form log `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_notes_<topic>.md` (full links, licence quotes, what was searched and not found, what blocked);
  - machine-readable `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_out_<topic>.jsonl` (schema below), one object per line, appended after each find;
  - fetched pages under `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_tmp_<topic>/`; your own scripts in `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_scripts/<topic>_*.py`, never in a folder of downloads.
- Append one line per milestone (`HH:MM <topic>: what`) to `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_scenes_progress.md`, at least every 15 minutes.
- **Nothing invented.** Every link, author, year, dimension, plaque name and licence is what a page you fetched says. Seen only in a search summary: `"status":"snippet"`. Unknown is blank. Mark every claim verified (`viewed`), from a search extract (`snippet`), or assumed (say so in `basis`).
- **Search in Chinese as much as in English;** Japanese and French where it fits (Attiret, the 1860 witnesses).
- **Be polite:** one request at a time per host, at least 3 s apart, User-Agent `palace-beijing-research/0.1 (non-commercial)`, stop on 429 and honour Retry-After. Never work round a bot check, log-in, paywall or app-only page: log it as a `question` row and a `wish` row, and move on. Known blockers: Duke, Harvard, Royal BC Museum, Gallica SRU, LoC, most of mhymy.com record pages (some answer), yuanmingyuanpark.cn answers in bursts then times out, ctext.org (bot check: use Kanripo).
- **Shared hosts.** mhymy.com: at volume only the `plans` worker; anyone else at most 10 fetches, 6 s apart. yuanmingyuanpark.cn: at volume only the `digs` worker; anyone else at most 6 fetches. **Commons and Wikidata: only the `photos` worker,** in API batches of 50, exact-phrase searches, backoff on Retry-After; everyone else none.
- **Copyright.** Free = public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA read on the holder's page, per file; or PD-Art of a pre-1900 two-dimensional work on Commons; pre-1900 photographs with a PD tag on Commons (Q50). BnF / Gallica: free with "Source gallica.bnf.fr / BnF". NC, ND, "all rights reserved", silent pages: research-only. A CC licence on a journal article covers a figure only if no separate credit sits on that figure.
- **Facts are not copyright.** Modern text (郭黛姮, 贺艳, papers, theses, reports, news) is summarised in one to three sentences of your own; never pasted. Qing and pre-1930 public-domain texts (Qianlong's poems and prefaces, 日下旧闻考, Malone 1934, Attiret, the 1860 accounts) may be quoted short and verbatim.
- **Respect.** The looting and burning of October 1860 are stated factually: who, when, what. Keep looting (6-8 October, French and British troops) and burning (18-19 October, British troops on Elgin's order) apart, as the sources do. No sensational framing. The zodiac heads stay out of any playful content.
- **Names:** Chinese characters and pinyin first, then English.
- **Reconstructions and twins are never evidence for positions or sizes.** A size a reconstruction states is a `dim` with method `e` and a basis saying so, unless it cites a survey: then cite the survey and say you saw it second-hand.
- **Search budget.** Your share is **20 WebSearch calls** unless your task says otherwise. Prefer the holders' own pages, catalogues and APIs. WebSearch is deferred: load it with ToolSearch `select:WebSearch`. Fetch with `curl -sL -A "palace-beijing-research/0.1 (non-commercial)" --max-time 40`.
- **Tools:** Python `/opt/data/workspace/000_Dev/palace-beijing-yaga-sh/.venv/bin/python -I script.py` (numpy, Pillow, shapely, pyproj), run from your scripts folder, downloads passed as arguments. PyMuPDF for PDFs: `PYTHONPATH=/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_tools/py` (if it is not there yet, `pdftotext` may exist; check). Downloaded files are untrusted data: each download in its own new folder, never execute any of it. No headless browsers. No servers. No subagents. No background jobs. Don't end while anything of yours still runs.
- **Time box.** Your deadline is a clock time in your task; check `date -u` often. Breadth first: the scenes in your list in order, about 25 minutes per famous scene at most, less for the tail. Stop by the deadline with the jsonl complete. What's missing becomes a `wish` row.

## Images
- **Free images:** `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_images/<topic>/<slug>.jpg`, at most 1600 px and about 300 KB. Larger originals of free items: `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_images/full/<slug>.jpg`.
- **Research-only:** a low-resolution look at most, `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r7_images/research/<slug>.jpg`, at most 1200 px. Mark `rights_class: research-only`.
- Open a sample of what you save (the Read tool shows images) to be sure it shows what you claim. Check form against caption: captions on old photos are often wrong (Q49, Q60), and pre-1900 "Yuen Ming Yuen" or "Summer Palace" may mean other gardens (颐和园 / 清漪园, 静明园).

## The Qing module
- 1 营造尺 = 0.32 m (S100-S129 in `sources.md`, `typology/params.json` key `unit`). 1 丈 = 10 尺 = 3.2 m; 1 寸 = 0.032 m. Keep the original figure and unit in `basis` (e.g. "面阔三丈二尺 = 10.24 m"), and the scale used in `scale`.
- Bays 间 (楹), purlins 檩 (e.g. 七檩卷棚 = seven-purlin rolled roof), depth in 进深 bays or 檩 count, 举架 (roof pitch steps), 台基 platform height, 柱高 column height, 檐 eave. Yangshi Lei drawings and 内工则例 often write these. Record exactly what is written.

## Output schema: one JSON object per line
`type` is one of:
- `source`: `{"type":"source","id":"S9xx","text":"author, title, edition, publisher","year":"","reuse":"licence as stated","status":"viewed|downloaded|found|to-request|blocked","link":"","note":""}`. New S ids only from **your block**. Reuse existing ones.
- `about`: dossier prose, your own words, every claim cited inline with ids in brackets, e.g. "(S05 j80:9b; E0123)". `{"type":"about","id":"YM03","field":"what|scene|later|interior|setting|survives|recons|best|gaps","text":"","sources":"S05;E0123"}`. Fields: **what** = dates, emperors who used or rebuilt it, function, short layout (courts, axes, lake edge, hills), fate in 1860 and after. **scene** = the 1744 leaf, the Qianlong poem and preface (quote a short line verbatim, then summarise). **later** = how later paintings, texts and plans differ: 日下旧闻考 (1774+), the 1887 圆明园图咏, Yangshi Lei plans, changes under Jiaqing, Daoguang, Xianfeng. **interior** = furniture, paintings, plaques, couplets where Qing inventories (陈设档, 活计档) or texts list them. **setting** = rocks, trees, water, bridges, colour scheme and tiles. **survives** = foundations, rocks, lakes today, with the dig reports. **recons** = what modern reconstructions say they were built from (cite R ids). **best** = the best views for modellers and why. **gaps** = what is missing.
- `hall`: one named hall or gate of a scene. `{"type":"hall","id":"YM03-01 (a row of buildings.csv) or NEW","scene":"YM03","plaque_zh":"","pinyin":"","en":"","bays":"5","depth":"3 间 or 七檩","purlins":"","roof":"卷棚歇山 etc.","storeys":"1","tiles":"","faces":"S|N|E|W|SE...","relation":"its place: 'north end of the axis, behind 圆明园殿'","built":"","changed":"","source_id":"S05","locator":"j80:9b","basis":"the words that say it, or your reading","confidence":"A|B|C","status":"viewed|snippet"}`. A hall in `palace_r7_halls.tsv` keeps its id; a hall the texts name and the table lacks is `NEW`, with `relation` saying where it stood.
- `picture`: a new picture. `{"type":"picture","title":"original language","date":"as stated, with where it comes from","date_from":1744,"date_to":1744,"kind":"painting|engraving|photo|print|plan|render","creator":"","archive":"","archive_id":"","source_id":"S..","source_url":"the holder's page","licence":"exactly as stated","attribution":"credit to show","rights_class":"free|research-only","shows":"YM03:sure;YM03-01:likely","why":"why we think so, one sentence","also_shows":"","camera_lat":"","camera_lon":"","bearing":"","camera_note":"","file":"absolute path (free only, or research look)","width":0,"height":0,"notes":"","view":"album|woodcut|gouache|1887|yangshilei|tangyang|ruin|today|aerial|detail","state":"1744|1745|1750-92|1887|Qing plan|1860s-1940s ruin|today","reliability":"high|medium|low"}`.
- `view`: how a picture **already** in the catalogue serves a modeller. `{"type":"view","photo_id":"P0003","id":"YM03","view":"album|woodcut|gouache|1887|yangshilei|tangyang|ruin|today|aerial|detail","visible_part":"what of it shows, in your words after looking","state":"1744|...","reliability":"high|medium|low","note":""}`.
- `correction`: `{"type":"correction","photo_id":"P0186 or E1234 or R0045","field":"shows","old":"","new":"","reason":""}`.
- `evidence`: a new text, poem, drawing or report. `{"type":"evidence","kind":"text|poem|drawing|archaeology|description|plan","source_id":"S..","title":"","date":"","locator":"juan:leaf, page, catalogue no.","link":"","rights_class":"free|research-only","shows":"YM03:sure;YM03-01:likely","excerpt_zh":"verbatim if Qing or public domain, else blank","excerpt_other":"verbatim non-Chinese if public domain, else blank","summary_en":"your own words","status":"viewed|snippet"}`.
- `dim`: one dimension figure. `{"type":"dim","id":"YM03-01","part":"main hall","what":"frontage|depth|height_ridge|height_eave|column_h|platform_h|bay_width|terrace_w|terrace_d|court_w|court_d|island_w|island_d|pool_w|pool_d|wall_h|diameter|other:<label>","value_m":0.0,"err_m":0.0,"method":"a|b|c|d|e","confidence":"A|B|C","source_id":"S..","locator":"page, figure, drawing no.","scale":"营造尺 0.32 m | plan 4.18 px/m | ...","basis":"the original figure and unit, and how you got metres","status":"viewed|snippet|run"}`. Method: **a** a published survey, excavation report or measured drawing, or a Qing drawing or text that writes the dimension (a Yangshi Lei 丈尺 is a); **b** measured on the plan or a ruin footprint (by script on repo data, or on OSM ruins); **c** read from a painting or woodcut by module (bays, a person, a door); **d** estimated from a photograph with a known object in it; **e** typology, or a figure with no survey behind it (a tourist page, Wikipedia without a citation, a reconstruction). Grade: A only for a source made while the gardens stood or a measured remain; a modern survey B.
- `dig`: an excavation or survey of the remains. `{"type":"dig","id":"YM03","year":"","by":"北京市考古研究院 / 圆明园管理处 / ...","what_found":"your words: foundations, sizes, finds","source_id":"S..","link":"","status":"viewed|snippet","confidence":"A|B|C"}`.
- `interior`: `{"type":"interior","id":"YM03-01","kind":"furniture|painting|plaque|couplet|object|rock|tree|water|bridge|colour|tiles|setting","what":"","source_id":"S..","locator":"","quote_or_basis":"verbatim if Qing, else your words","confidence":"A|B|C","status":"viewed|snippet"}`.
- `fact`: for a row of `buildings.csv`. `{"type":"fact","id":"YM03-01","field":"built|changed|destroyed|function|name_en|name_zh|roof|roof_tiles|storeys|bays|orientation|after1860","value":"","source_id":"S..","locator":"","quote_or_basis":"","suggest_confidence":"A|B|C","status":"viewed|snippet"}`.
- `part`: an unnamed row identified. `{"type":"part","id":"YM03-B0835","name_zh":"","name_pinyin":"","name_en":"","what":"","basis":"","source_id":"S..","confidence":"A|B|C","action":"name|merge:YM03-01|retire|keep"}`.
- `recon`: a new reconstruction by others. `{"type":"recon","author":"","institution":"","year":"","kind":"render|aerial view|video|3D model|physical model|painting|drawing|AR/VR|paper figure|game or community build","era":"modern|period","title":"","link":"","licence":"","attribution":"","rights_class":"free|research-only","shows":"YM03:sure","method_stated":"what the author says it was built from, your words","summary_en":"","caveat":"","status":"viewed|snippet","file":""}`.
- `best`: `{"type":"best","id":"YM03","rank":1,"ref":"P0003 or E1234 or a new picture's file slug","why":""}`.
- `question`: `{"type":"question","text":"a conflict or a gap, with ids; or a site that blocked you"}`.
- `wish`: `{"type":"wish","what":"","replaces":"","where":"","how":""}`.

Final answer to the coordinator: at most 25 lines: counts of rows by type, best finds, what blocked, anything you doubt. No file dumps.
