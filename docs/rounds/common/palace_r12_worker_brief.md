# Round 12 (palace_r12_greens): research workers' common brief

Project: walkable 3D reconstruction of the Old Summer Palace 圆明园 before October 1860, repo `/opt/data/workspace/000_Dev/palace-beijing-yaga-sh` (READ ONLY for you: never write in the repo, never run git). Rules in its `CLAUDE.md` (read the "Research rules" and "Copyright" sections only). This round adds the gardens' vegetation: trees, bamboo, lotus, reeds, lawns, flower beds, and the rockeries 假山 / garden rocks. Your rows become `research/greens/species.csv`; the coordinator merges them.

## What to deliver (only in /opt/data/cache/scratch/)
1. `palace_r12_greens_rows_<topic>.csv` (your topic is in your task), UTF-8, header exactly:
   `scene_id,plant_zh,latin,name_en,role,place,place_text,season,source_kind,sources,locator,quote,confidence,status,use,reject_reason,note`
   One row per (scene, plant, place) that a source supports. Several sources for the same thing: one row, `sources` joined by `;`.
   - `scene_id`: an id from `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r12_greens_in/scenes.csv` (YM01..YM88, CC01..CC30, QC01..QC82, XY01..XY15). A hall-level id (`YM03-11`) is allowed if the source places it at one hall. `GARDEN-YM` / `GARDEN-CC` / `GARDEN-QC` / `GARDEN-ALL` for garden-wide statements.
   - `plant_zh`: the plant as the source names it (松, 柳, 荷, 竹, 桃, 杏, 梅, 海棠, 牡丹, 玉兰, 梧桐, 槐, 柏, 丁香, 菊, 兰, 芦苇, 菱, 稻...). `latin`, `name_en`: your identification (e.g. 松 in Beijing gardens = mostly Pinus tabuliformis, Chinese pine; 白皮松 Pinus bungeana; 柏 = Platycladus orientalis or Juniperus chinensis; 柳 = Salix babylonica / S. matsudana; 荷 = Nelumbo nucifera; 竹 in Beijing = Phyllostachys spp., e.g. P. propinqua / P. aureosulcata; 梧桐 = Firmiana simplex; 桐 may be Paulownia; 杏 = Prunus armeniaca; 桃 = Prunus persica; 梅 = Prunus mume (tender in Beijing, often potted: say so); 海棠 = Malus spectabilis / M. micromalus; 玉兰 = Magnolia denudata; 槐 = Styphnolobium japonicum; 丁香 = Syringa oblata; 牡丹 = Paeonia suffruticosa). Note doubts in `note`.
   - `role`, one of: avenue, grove, specimen, hedge, lotus, reed, lawn, bamboo, rockery_plant, flower_bed, orchard, climber, aquatic, field, potted.
   - `place`, one of (controlled, the generator reads it): `scene` (anywhere in the scene), `north`, `south`, `east`, `west` (that side of the scene's island or compound), `front_court`, `rear_court`, `court` (inside the courts between halls), `around_hall` (beside a named hall: give the hall's id or name in `place_text`), `shore` (the water's edge), `island`, `hill` (on its hills / mounds), `hill_foot`, `dike` (on a causeway or dike), `along_way` (lining a path), `pond` (on the water: lotus, reeds, water plants), `lake_edge` (in the shallow water at the edge: reeds), `bridge` (beside a bridge), `rockery` (on or by a rockery), `field`, `outside_wall`. `place_text`: the source's own words or your reading of the leaf ("both sides of the bridge", "behind the hall, on the rock slope").
   - `season`: blossom / leaf season if the source says (spring, summer, autumn, winter, all); blank if not stated.
   - `source_kind`: poem (a poem's verse), preface (the prose preface of the 1744 poem), text (日下旧闻考 and other Qing prose), leaf (read off a picture: the 1744 album, an engraving, a painting), archive (a court record, cited through a modern work), survivor (a living old tree or rock in the park today), account (a visitor's description, 1743-1860), study (a modern scholar's statement), typology (typical, no direct source: allowed only when marked as such).
   - `sources`: S ids already in the repo (`research/sources.md`: S05 = 日下旧闻考, S40 = the 1744 poems with prefaces, S08 = the 1744 album, S360/S361 = the park's compilations of imperial poems, research-only: cite facts, quote nothing longer than a phrase). A NEW source: give it an id from your block (in your task) and add a line for it in your notes file in the same format as `research/sources.md` rows: `| Sxxxx | title, author | date | licence | status (viewed / found / snippet) | URL. note |`.
   - `locator`: juan:leaf, page, poem title, picture id (P0001...), with where in the picture ("left third, behind the hall").
   - `quote`: the Chinese words verbatim for public-domain Qing texts (short: the clause that names the plant); for research-only modern works a short summary in English, not a quote.
   - `confidence`: A = a source made while the gardens stood (1744 poems, prefaces and album, 日下旧闻考 1780s, Qing court records, Attiret 1743, accounts by eyewitnesses of 1860 before the burning, the Xiyang Lou engravings 1780s) and naming THIS scene; B = a later source (a ruin photo, a modern study, a survivor tree whose age is only estimated), or a written description only; C = typical, inferred.
   - `status`: verified (you read the words / looked at the picture yourself), snippet (only a search summary), assumed.
   - `use`: keep / reject. Reject, with `reject_reason`, a plant that does not suit (subtropical species that cannot stand outdoors in Beijing winters: e.g. 芭蕉 banana is grown in Beijing gardens only with winter protection, palms, citrus outdoors; a plant named only in a metaphor or a literary allusion, e.g. 桃源 as a name only, 蟾宫桂 the moon's cassia; a plant of a later period than 1860 with no earlier attestation). A potted plant is `potted`, keep it but say so.
2. `palace_r12_greens_notes_<topic>.md`: what you read, what you found, the new source lines, open doubts (each a candidate question for `research/questions.md`), and one line per milestone with the UTC time.

## Rules
- Never invent a source, a number, a page or a licence. Mark every row verified / snippet / assumed.
- Respect copyright: summarise modern books and papers; quote only Qing texts and pre-1900 public-domain works.
- Keep the history factual and respectful (1860 is a national wound in China; no cute framing).
- Do not write in the repo. Do not start servers or browsers. Stop at your deadline even if not finished, and say what is left.
- Final reply: a short summary (rows written, by confidence, the most useful findings for the 3D view, the rejected plants, the new sources). The coordinator reads your files, not a long reply.
