## The lake, in general (verified on the overview crop ymf_over)
- 福海 sits roughly in x 5530-8165, y 1650-4160 (plan px). The shores are drawn as single lines. The way round it runs on drawn land strips (dikes): the north strip (YM31, between 福海 and a long pond), the east strip (YM36/YM33, between 福海 and an east water), the south strip (YM35, between 福海 and the south waters, cut by the 夹镜鸣琴 bridge BR027 over the outlet) and the west strip (YM30, between 福海 and a lagoon, cut by the bridge pavilion BR028).
