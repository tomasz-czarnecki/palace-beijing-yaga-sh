## 2. Web, as I went (19:30-19:41)

- **如园 延清堂 (S362 = 如园延清堂建筑复原研究, park research report, 2024-03-28):** read in full (downloaded to scratch `palace_r9_remains_dl/ruyuan.txt`). The best figures of the round for steps and a landing, all excavated (A):
  - landing 码头 north of the north terrace: 5.1 m E-W x 3.35 m N-S, 1.1 m below the terrace; from Jiaqing one came to 如园 by water here, and in Daoguang-Xianfeng it replaced the gate as the main entrance (verified, quoted in the CSV).
  - front steps 垂带踏跺 4.6 x 2.1 m, four treads 0.16 m high, 0.416 m deep; side steps 抄手踏跺 of four treads on both terraces (from the Yangshi Lei drawing); cloud steps 云步踏跺 of unworked Taihu rock at the four corners.
  - apron 散水 of thin uneven stone slabs, continuous round hall and terraces; garden roads reddened by fire; the north terrace lies under the modern ring road.
  - drawings used: 如园[地盘全样] 样385-0015 and seven 延清堂 sheets (NLC), published in 国家图书馆藏样式雷图档·圆明园卷续编 第八函.
- **Bridges (NEW: 清华同衡, 圆明园考古遗址公园 历史桥梁形制研究, 2017-09, 238 pp. PDF on the park site):** downloaded (420 MB, in scratch `palace_r9_remains_dl/bridges.pdf`, text `bridges.txt`). For each bridge it gives the plans that draw it, the type "考古资料显示", and what survives. Remains in place: granite 条石 abutments 桥基 at 九洲清晏 南大桥 and 如意桥, 碧澜桥 (杏花春馆), 鸣玉溪桥 (长春仙馆), 万方安和 万1 and 万3, 碧桐书院 碧1 and 碧2; timber piles at 九洲清晏 (24, 8, 4), 上下天光 (13; 62 for the zigzag bridge 上3), 茹古涵今 (16), 万方安和 (8, 8), 月地云居 (2), 天然图画 (4). No widths in metres anywhere in it. Totals: 190 bridges, 6 in place in original form. Its bridge numbers are its own (九1 ...); they are not the repo's BR ids: the coordinator must match them by scene and side.
- **Docks (NEW: 北京圆明园研究会, 圆明三园中的船坞, mhymy xsjl/6203, 2021):** nine docks, quotes from Qing archives (one dock size in 丈尺: 安澜园 columns). Rows RM in the CSV.
- **大宫门:** four phases of digs 2012-18 found 朝房, 水房, river, roads, square, 金水桥; no widths found. The Beijing heritage bureau doc (wwj.beijing.gov.cn 2016 no.200) is only the request for phase 3 (2,000 m2): no findings.
- **文源阁 2024:** news.cn 2024-09-23 captions: 宫门, 御路, 河道, drains, 蹬道 steps; no widths.
- **Not Yuanmingyuan, do not use:** sina 2004-09-10 news is the 南越国 palace in Guangzhou (a 3 m walk, a 6 m gallery): unrelated. The sohu "九洲池" article is Luoyang.
- **Rules note:** I ran four `?s=` searches on mhymy.com before reading its robots.txt, which disallows `/?s=*`. I stopped at once, deleted the result files, and used only the article URL it led to (`/xsjl/6203.html`, allowed). No further mhymy searches.
- 含经堂 brief S143: confirmed as 考古 2004 no.2 pp.41-65 (靳枫毅, 王继红 et al.) by a library record; abstract only (site over 60,000 m2 on an island, south square area, main hall court, residential north, N-S axis); no path widths seen.
