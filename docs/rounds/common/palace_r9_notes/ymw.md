## Main doubts (for the coordinator)
1. Double lines: I read narrow double lines as water except where a pailou or bridge makes them a street (YM45 street, YM15 马道, YM64). The "closed ends at a gap" drawing (文源阁 gate, 大北门, 花神庙, YM70) is read as a land crossing.
2. Dots: at 文源阁 and 紫碧山房 the plan draws dotted lines through the rockery and round the pond; I traced them as paths (B). They could be tree rows.
3. 曲院风荷 dikes and 万方安和 links depend on which side of a line is water; I give my reasoning in the notes of each row.
4. The long one-bay rows on both sides of the street south of 双桥 (YMW-103 and the matching row in YM44) may be shop rows of a 买卖街 rather than galleries; the name 买卖街 is not written on the plan there.
5. 万方安和: on the plan the 卍 hall is reached by two very short links (north-west and north-east arms), no long bridge; boat access assumed but not drawn.
