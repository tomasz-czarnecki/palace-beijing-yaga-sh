# Round 9 album reader: Forty Scenes leaves (圆明园四十景图, 1744, BnF, public domain)

All rows grade A (drawn on a leaf of 1744), status verified unless the note says assumed. Leaf = P00NN = YMNN. Images 1920 px wide, read at that size. "Left/right/top/bottom" are of the leaf as stored.

## YM01 正大光明 (P0001)
Entry from the south: a broad grey road with a light central strip (out of frame from 大宫门) crosses the curving moat on a flat stone bridge to 出入贤良门 (steps before its door), then a broad paved court with a central 甬道 leads straight to the steps of 正大光明殿; behind the hall only rockery and hills (寿山), then the lake beyond. Reached by land. A walled lane leads off north-west to the office courts. Only one bridge is drawn on the axis (the text's "three bridges" not visible). verified.

## YM03 九洲清晏 (P0003)
Seen from the south over 前湖: the island is reached by land bridges, a dog-leg red-railed bridge at the south-east (likely 如意桥) and a short bridge at the south-west edge (likely 南大桥), plus a raised bridge on the west channel (likely 棕亭桥); a pale shore path runs along the south front. A gate with red doors in the front wall opens on white slanting stairs and the axis courts, joined by covered galleries; the north side opens on 后湖 (no landing clearly drawn). Bridge-to-id matches are assumed.

## YM02 勤政亲贤 (P0002)
From the open forecourt along the south a gate in the long front row leads into a dense grid of walled courts joined by galleries; walled lanes (夹道) run north on the west and east sides to the rear courts; behind are hills and a lake with a red-railed stepped structure (unidentified). Reached by land; nothing by water is drawn for the compound itself.

## YM32 蓬岛瑶台 (P0032)
An island in open water (福海): the only way in is by boat to white landing steps that rise straight into the front gate of the south row; a paved court leads to the steps of the main hall. Two red-railed bridges lead on: a bent bridge north-west to the north islet with its walled hall, and a low flat bridge east to the willow islet with the hexagonal pavilion 瀛海仙山亭. No boat is drawn. Reached by boat.

## YM29 方壶胜境 (P0029)
Approached over the water from the south (福海 bay): the front pavilion stands on a cross-shaped marble terrace with broad flights of steps down into the water, flanked by two pavilions on their own terraces; from there stairs climb the tiered terraces to the two-storey front hall and the rear halls. By land a path along the west shore (figures walking), below a zig-zag mountain gallery with pavilions, crosses a white hump bridge to the west end of the terrace. A large boat house roof (船坞) stands at the right; a small plank bridge serves a south-east shore hall. Reached by boat (landing steps) and by land from the west.

## YM31 平湖秋月 (P0031)
Seen from 福海: a row of halls on the north shore with a shore path in front, a small red-railed jetty at the west end, and a white multi-arched bridge over an inlet that leads east to a tower on a high terrace climbed by stairs; a pavilion over the western stream. A visitor could arrive by boat at the jetty or walk the shore from the west; the bridge carries the shore way on eastward. Reached by land along the shore and by boat.

## YM20 澹泊宁静 (P0020)
A lone large hall (田字殿) on a lawn above a pond, with small landing steps into the water before it; a water pavilion on posts with a red-railed stair at the west shore, paddy fields with bunds behind, two small halls with a trellis to the east. No paved ways or galleries are drawn: one walks across lawn and along the shore. Reached by boat (landing steps) or along the shore from the west.

## YM13 万方安和 (P0013)
The 卍-shaped hall stands in the lake on a white base with stepped edges (boat landings assumed). By land one comes from the south along a long narrow dike with rocks, past a red-railed bridge at its foot, to a white bridge/stair that enters the hall's east wing; a second red-railed bridge lies on the north side, others on the west shore; the cross pavilion 十字亭 stands on the south shore path. Reached by land over the dike and the east bridge, and by boat.

## YM09 杏花春馆 (P0009)
Entered from the south over a stone bridge with railings across the stream, then a broad pale path winds between two hills into an open village ground of vegetable plots, scattered cottages, a well pavilion and the tavern with its banner pole; a pavilion crowns the hill behind. No courts, galleries or paved ways: earth paths and plot bunds. Reached by land over the bridge (the stream could also carry boats; none drawn).

## YM14 武陵春色 (P0014)
A closed valley among hills with a winding stream and peach trees: from rocks at the foreground (approach to the 石洞 assumed) one comes to a walled compound with long galleries, then follows the stream, crosses a short red-railed bridge and climbs to an upper compound with a fence. Paths are bank paths, not paved. Reached by land through the hills (the stream may also have carried boats, as the Peach Blossom Spring story implies; none drawn).

## YM15 山高水长 (P0015)
A two-storey hall faces a vast open lawn to the west, with a grey paved terrace before it; small halls step down to the south along the lawn's edge, and service rows with a walled lane lie behind to the east. One reaches it by land; the lawn is open ground, no paths drawn on it.

## YM38 坐石临流 (P0038)
The leaf is dominated by a long straight street (read as the 买卖街 shop street) with a light central strip, rising from the lake shore between rows of shop halls to the arched gate of a walled city (read as 舍卫城); galleries run beside it and lanes open off it. At the west a red-railed bridge crosses the stream from the hills, and a small hexagonal pavilion by the water is taken to be 坐石临流亭 (assumed). Reached by land along the shore, perhaps also by boat to the street end (not drawn).

## YM39 曲院风荷 (P0039)
The lake is crossed by the long white nine-arch bridge 九孔桥, with a red timber archway at the west end and a pavilion or second archway at the east end; shore paths run round to a walled compound with a gate tower on the north shore. In the foreground the hall 麴院风荷殿 sits by the water with a small white bridge beside it, and a bridge with blue-white railings crosses a channel to the east shore. Reached by land, over the bridges and along the shores.

## YM33 接秀山房 (P0033)
Halls strung along the steep east shore of 福海: from a red-railed bridge at the south a shore path runs past a pavilion to a railed waterside terrace and up the shore through walled halls to a tower, from which a railed gallery runs along the ridge to the northern halls. A valley path enters from the east between the hills. Reached by land along the shore and down the valley; the waterside terrace suggests boat arrival too (not drawn).

## YM04 镂月开云 (P0004)
From the south a red-railed bridge leads over the water into a peony garden of beds and rocks, up to the glazed front hall on a stepped platform; behind it a walled court with a gateway opens to the rear halls joined by galleries. A pavilion stands on the west shore. Reached by land over the south bridge.

## YM05 天然图画 (P0005)
Halls on the east shore of 后湖 round a lawn and a pond with a pine islet: a broad external stair climbs a terrace to the upper floor of the two-storey hall at the left, joined by upper galleries to the hall behind; a front hall stands before a long lattice wall enclosing a bamboo court. Reached by land along the shore; the lake is beyond (no landing drawn).

## YM06 碧桐书院 (P0006)
A walled compound in a hollow of hills: from a red-railed wooden bridge in the foreground a bank path leads to the front hall in the south wall, then paved walks lead through stepped walled courts to the main and rear halls; a waterfall falls on the hill behind. Reached by land over the south bridge.

## YM07 慈云普护 (P0007)
On the north shore of 后湖: a multi-span wooden bridge at the left leads into a flower garden before the front hall (trellis porch); from the court a long covered gallery climbs diagonally to the two-storey hall, beside which stands the three-storey bell tower; a small pavilion stands at the shore. Reached by land over the west bridge, possibly by boat at the shore pavilion.

## YM08 上下天光 (P0008)
On the north shore of 后湖: a long zig-zag wooden bridge on piles with railings runs out over the water from the west shore past a square pavilion to the water terrace of the two-storey hall, then on past a hexagonal pavilion back to the east shore; a short red stair from the terrace serves as a boat landing. A path climbs behind to a walled rear group. Reached by boat (landing) or over the zig-zag bridges from either shore.

## YM10 坦坦荡荡 (P0010)
From the stream shore one enters by the red-doored hall in the long front row into a court dominated by a large stone-walled sunken fish pool; a walk runs round the top of its walls to the hall at the north and a pavilion at the pool; a gallery runs east to a pavilion and halls. A small red-railed bridge among rocks crosses the stream at the east (likely 碧澜桥). Reached by land.

## YM11 茹古涵今 (P0011)
A walled compound on an island-like ground between streams: red-railed bridges enter at the south-east and west, a shore path runs along the front, a long front row opens into galleried courts that step back to the two-storey hall with a pyramidal roof. Reached by land over bridges.

## YM12 长春仙馆 (P0012)
A large grid of walled courts with narrow lanes, on ground ringed by streams: red-railed bridges enter at the south-east, the front and the west; a gate in the long front row leads in. Behind, a white bridge carrying a pavilion crosses the water to the north (perhaps 鸣玉溪桥). Reached by land over bridges.

## YM16 月地云居 (P0016)
A Buddhist temple with red walls: a meadow path from the south-west reaches a red-railed bridge over the stream, then the temple gate with steps; the axis runs north through red-walled courts to the square double-roofed front hall, the main hall and the rear tower. Smaller walled courts lie to the east. Reached by land over the bridge.

## YM17 鸿慈永祜 (安佑宫) (P0017)
The ancestral temple: from the lake-shore path one turns into a pine grove past a group of red timber archways, then crosses white marble bridges over the moat (月河) between two tall archways to the triple gate in the red wall; a paved axis runs through the courts, past corner stele pavilions, to the yellow-glazed main hall on its white stepped terrace. Reached by land.

## YM18 汇芳书院 (P0018)
Inside the north garden wall: a compound of courts joined by long one-storey galleries, with a two-storey crescent-shaped gallery (read as 眉月轩) curving along a pond to a small pavilion; a two-tier pavilion stands at the front corner; a small hall with a stepped base stands at the water below a rock hill to the east. Walking is along the galleries; reached by land (no bridge to the compound drawn).

## YM19 日天琳宇 (P0019)
Temples on the north shore: a west group of two-storey blocks joined at upper level, climbed by a broad external stair; an east temple with a red front wall, a gate between two tall flag poles and a diagonal stair rising inside to rear halls on higher ground; at the far right a stepped way with lamp posts climbs a valley. Reached by land along the shore.

## YM21 映水兰香 (P0021)
A farmland scene: a small red-railed bridge in the foreground, a shore path past an open pavilion, then open ground with paddy fields and bunds on the east and halls under pines and rocks on the west; a path runs on to halls at the back. Reached by land over the bridge; no paved ways.

## YM22 水木明瑟 (P0022)
From a red-railed bridge at the bottom left one crosses a lawn to the white stepped terrace of the front hall; a long covered gallery on a raised base runs to the rear hall; two small red bridges cross the channel feeding the water-driven hall to the east; a path runs along the stream past paddy fields to a pavilion. Reached by land over the bridge.

## YM23 濂溪乐处 (P0023)
A large lotus pond ringed by hills: the main group stands on an island joined to the west bank by short red-railed bridges, and from it one-storey railed galleries run out over the water in a rectangular loop; a single hall on a white base stands alone in the lotus water (boat access assumed); shore paths link pavilions round the pond. Reached by land over the west bridges, and by boat on the pond.

## YM24 多稼如云 (P0024)
A big lotus pond: a dike-like bank runs round its west and north sides from a plank bridge by a hall at the lower left, crossing a white hump bridge beside a small pavilion, to the main hall group on the north-east. Reached by land along the dike; the far lake behind holds islets of other scenes.

## YM25 鱼跃鸢飞 (P0025)
A two-storey square hall stands at the edge of a water channel, joined by a gallery to a walled front group reached by a path winding up between hills; a small white railed bridge crosses the water to its east; fields with bunds lead west to a village (北远山村 beyond). Reached by land.

## YM26 北远山村 (P0026)
A village of houses along a lane above a stream, with a small red-walled temple at its west end and a hall on a stepped terrace at the east; fields behind. In the foreground the stream passes through an arched opening in a stone wall (a water gate or an arched bridge). Reached by land along the lane; the stream may have carried boats (none drawn).

## YM27 西峰秀色 (P0027)
An island of dense courts joined by galleries, entered over a small red-railed bridge from the south, with a shore path round the low front hill to an open railed hall at the west corner; on the north shore stands a red open timber structure (landing or stair, unidentified), facing a rock hill with a waterfall across the water. Reached by land over the bridge, and by water to the north.

## YM28 四宜书屋 (P0028)
Halls on a shore with pines behind; a water pavilion at the west; a high arched bridge crosses the foreground channel; in an inlet two roofed boats are moored beside tall poles, next to a hall at the water's edge read as the boat house (船坞). Reached by boat (moored boats drawn) and by land over the arched bridge.

## YM30 澡身浴德 (P0030)
On the west shore of 福海: a long shore path runs from a hall group on a high stone base with a red landing stair at the water in the foreground, past a pavilion on a high terrace (stair at its side), to a north group with a gallery and a shore pavilion. Inland behind the hills a small red bridge leads to a walled court. Reached along the shore by land and by boat to the landing.

## YM34 别有洞天 (P0034)
A snow scene: a narrow water between hills, closed at the west by a crenellated wall with a gate (城关/水关), crossed at the east by a long railed bridge with a pavilion at mid-span; halls line both shores, a shore path runs along the north row, small red bridges sit high in the hills. Reached by land through the wall gate or over the east bridge, and by water through the water gate (assumed). The 石舫 is not identifiable.

## YM35 夹镜鸣琴 (P0035)
A dike with a path runs from the west hills between two waters to a white single-arch bridge carrying a pavilion (夹镜鸣琴桥亭); beyond it the cliff rises to a glazed temple with two banner poles (广育宫), whose ascent is not drawn. A small red bridge sits in the west hills; halls with a gate lie at the bottom shore. Reached by land along the dike and over the bridge.

## YM36 涵虚朗鉴 (P0036)
On the east shore of 福海: a red-railed bridge at the bottom left starts a shore path that runs up the shore past a two-storey hall, a railed waterside terrace and a hillside pavilion to a two-span red-railed bridge at the north-east corner, then on to the north shore halls. Reached by land along the shore; the waterside terrace could take a boat.

## YM37 廓然大公 (P0037)
A broad path winds from halls with a fence at the lower left between hills to the main walled group on the south shore of a lake; a shore path along the east water passes small halls and a two-storey waterside hall; stone steps climb to a hillside pavilion in the foreground; beyond the lake a rock hill carries a pavilion. Reached by land.

## YM40 洞天深处 (P0040)
The princes' quarters (四所): two blocks of walled courts laid out on a grid of straight paved lanes, a central north-south lane between the blocks, a lane along the south front inside the outer wall, and lanes on the west and east sides; each 所 opens its gates onto the lanes. A further walled group lies to the west with a small wooden bridge. Reached by land; the most clearly drawn network of paved ways in the album.

## XY01 谐奇趣 south (P0041)
From the pond a semicircular water step on the axis lands on the balustraded court terrace; a paved walk rings the lobed fountain basin; a double curving stair embracing the cascade climbs to the main hall's upper door; curved arcades sweep east and west to the octagonal music pavilions. Reached by boat from the south pond (landing drawn) or along the terrace from the west.

## XY01 谐奇趣 north (P0042)
The north side faces a formal garden: broad straight east-west walks between topiary, a circular patterned plaza with a fountain on the axis, then a double straight stair to the upper terrace. The axis continues north toward the viewer (花园门 side, assumed). Reached by land through the north garden.

## XY02 蓄水楼 east (P0043)
A straight slab-paved walk with cobbled borders runs through the topiary garden to a semicircular paving and the single door of the water tower; a panelled wall runs off north. Reached by land from the east garden (the walk comes from 谐奇趣's north garden side, assumed).

## XY03 花园门 north (P0044)
From the north bank a flat plank bridge with railings crosses the canal on the axis, then a slab walk leads to a semicircular apron and the baroque gate with lions in the long panelled wall of the maze garden; a stepped ramp descends into the canal at the right, by an outflow under the wall. Reached by land over the bridge from the north.

## XY04 黄花阵 (P0045)
From the canal bridge one passes the front gate (pillars with finials) into a small court closed by an arched screen and then into the maze of low carved walls, which leads by winding walks to the round platform with curved stairs on two sides and the domed central pavilion; a short walk leads on to a hall at the far end. Side gates east and west open on strips with streams, a small bridge and gate-houses in the outer walls. Reached by land over the bridge.

## XY05 养雀笼 west (P0046)
A straight slab walk through topiary parterres leads to a semicircular apron and the arched central door of the aviary gate-building, flanked by caged bays; a side hall stands to the north. The axis passes through the building. Reached by land from the west garden.

## XY05 养雀笼 east (P0047)
The axis leaves the aviary eastward through a wrought-iron grille gate in the baroque east front, down a short flight of low steps and across a balustraded stone bridge over a channel; topiary beds and balustraded terraces with urn-topped walls flank it. Reached by land; the bridge carries the axis east toward 方外观/海晏堂.

## XY06 方外观 (P0048)
A wide slab-paved bridge with scrolling parapets crosses the channel on the axis to semicircular steps and the ground-floor door; two curving balustraded stairs sweep up to the upper balcony; a small bridge leads west across the stream to a Chinese-roofed pavilion (五竹亭 side, assumed), a balustraded terrace lies east. Reached by land over the bridge from the south.

## XY07 五竹亭 north (P0049)
From the north a narrow paved walk runs between two raised flower beds to a round fountain basin and a semicircular court before five bamboo-lattice pavilions joined in an arc by low lattice galleries; an arched opening in the central pavilion carries the axis through to the south. Reached by land.

## XY08 海晏堂 west (P0050)
A slab walk on the axis crosses a large paved plaza to the basin of the water clock fountain (十二时辰水法, the twelve bronze figures on its sides); paved walks curve round it to two long balustraded stairs with landings that climb symmetrically to the central door; colonnaded galleries close the plaza at the sides. Reached by land from the west.

## XY08 海晏堂 north (P0051)
The long north wing (the reservoir block) faces a topiary garden with a grid of paved walks linking an octagonal figure fountain, a round railed basin and an octagonal statue basin; short flights of steps lead to its three doors; the main block joins at the right with a balustraded upper terrace. Reached by land through the north garden.

## XY08 海晏堂 east (P0052)
On the east the hall rises as a stepped terrace block: from a herringbone-paved court a slab walk leads to low steps and an arched ground-floor door; two pairs of straight balustraded stairs cross the east face in a zig-zag, outward to the first terrace and inward to the upper terrace before the east pavilion. Curving paved walks lead round the sides. Reached by land from the east court.

## XY08 海晏堂 south (P0053)
The south wing mirrors the north: a topiary garden with a grid of paved walks and three basins, short flights of steps at three doors, the main block joining at the left; a balustraded terrace at the east end. Reached by land.

## XY09 远瀛观 (P0054)
Seen from above the 大水法: two curving balustraded stairs rise from the fountain court to a broad raised slab terrace, across which a paved strip leads to two low steps and the central door with lions; the terrace is balustraded and walled from the lawns at the sides. Reached by land from the 大水法 court below (north-south axis).

## XY10 大水法 (P0055)
From the south a narrow paved walk runs on the axis between two parterres to the lobed basin before the great fountain screen; paved walks curve round to the two tiered obelisk fountains at the sides; high walls close the court east and west. 远瀛观 is hidden behind the screen (its stairs are on plate 14). Reached by land along the axis from 观水法.

## XY11 观水法 (P0056)
The throne court seen from the 大水法 side: a slab walk on the axis leads to a curved flight of steps and the throne platform before the carved screen; baroque gateways stand in the west and east walls, each with a broad flight of steps down into the court, joined by a curved paved band across it. Reached by land through the side gates (and from the 大水法 court on the axis).

## XY12 线法山门 (P0057)
A long straight narrow paved walk runs through a dense grove of clipped trees to the open central arch of a baroque triple gate between curving panelled walls; the axis continues through it toward 线法山. Reached by land on the east-west axis.

## XY12 线法山 (P0058)
A broad herringbone walk between topiary leads to an opening in a high clipped hedge-wall flanked by two urn posts; behind it a pale ramp road winds up the conical tree-covered hill to a round paved terrace and the octagonal pavilion on top; a ring path runs round the foot. Reached by land through 线法山门 on the axis. (The ramp is recorded as service_path, "HILL RAMP": an open road, not a covered gallery.)

## XY13 线法山东门 (P0059)
A broad herringbone walk between topiary and rockwork runs east to the open central arch of a baroque gate (side doors closed); through the arch the water of 方河 is seen. Reached by land on the axis from 线法山; beyond is water. Match of the plate to XY13 螺蛳牌楼 assumed.

## XY14/XY15 方河 and 线法墙 (P0060)
Seen from across the square pond: a broad flight of water steps on the axis rises from the pond to a paved east bank, behind which stand the perspective scenery walls (线法画), whose arches are painted, not real gates; the pond has stepped revetted corners. The axis of the Xiyang Lou ends here at water: arrival by boat across 方河 to the landing steps is possible, and the bank is walked along. The scenery is not a way through.

## Summary (19:45 UTC)
- Done: all 40 Forty Scenes leaves (P0001-P0040) and all 20 Xiyang Lou plates (P0041-P0060). 364 rows in `palace_r9_album.csv`.
- Method: each leaf's painted half (the right half of the scan, the left half holds the text) was cropped to scratch (`palace_r9_albumcrops/`, about 940 px wide) and read; zooms at full size for YM01, YM03, YM29, YM32, YM38. Plates read at 2400 px. No leaf too large or too small.
- Kind `gate` is not in the allowed list, so 28 gate rows are kind `courtyard_path` with `what_is_shown` starting "GATE OPENING:". The 线法山 ramp is `service_path` "HILL RAMP".
- Ids: hall ids are matched from rows.csv by name and position; anything marked "assumed" in the note is an inference about which hall or bridge it is. Bridge ids BR001/002/005 (YM03), BR003 (YM10), BR004 (YM12), BR006 (YM01), BR014 (YM17), BR027 (YM35) are matched by position or name, mostly assumed.
- Doubts: leaves are bird's-eye paintings, not plans: lengths and which hall a gallery reaches are often hidden by trees; paths on lawns are often not drawn at all (YM15, YM20). YM38's street and walled city are read as 买卖街 and 舍卫城 from knowledge of the scene, not from rows.csv. YM31/YM33/YM36 waterside terraces are possible landings, not certain. The XY plate 19 is matched to XY13 螺蛳牌楼 by assumption.
