## The 1856 route (E0381) and 丽春门
- 丽春门 is not on the plan's gate list. Candidates, neither label matching: G18 秀?村门 (8050,4620), the passage through the double wall between Yuanming Yuan and Qichun, closest to 福海 and to a 雷峰夕照 landing; G22 绮园门 (9645,4725), the north gate of Qichun, with the dike QC-089 leading in from it. The text's order (landing on 福海 → 丽春门 → ride along the old garden's east wall → past 敷春堂 → 西大堤 → dismount at 含晖楼) is better served by a gate on the Yuanming Yuan side (G18) than by G22, but this is assumed only.
- The route then needs a ride east-west across Qichun: past 敷春堂 (QC04, east) and the 西大堤 to 含晖楼 (QC13, west) and its riding ground 撒马道 (QC-058). The straight N-S strip west of 畅和堂 (QC-066) is the only dike-like strip I read in the west; not proved to be the 西大堤.
