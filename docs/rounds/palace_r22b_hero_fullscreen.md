## Overrides
1. **The hero is the picture.** Full-bleed, edge to edge, the carousel image fills the entire hero (height `100svh` minus the header, min about 620 px, `object-fit: cover`), like Küstrin's hero. NO framed leaf, NO small picture beside the text. Keep the light, painted site: light header on top, and at the hero's foot a soft paper-coloured ink-wash fade or hills edge (a thin SVG) into the next light band. The 1744 leaf moves to the intro band as a quiet framed evidence picture with its credit line.
2. **Text over the picture** (headline 圆明园 / Yuanmingyuan / "The Old Summer Palace before October 1860", the research chip, two buttons, the seal stamp): lower-left, on a gentle scrim so it stays legible on every slide (WCAG AA, measure it on the brightest slide; a soft dark-to-transparent gradient from the bottom-left, or a frosted paper-white card, your choice, but the picture must stay visible and beautiful; do not cover more than about a third of it). Headline in light ink on the scrim or ink on a paper card: whichever tests AA on all slides. Do not make the page dark: only the hero picture is rich; everything under it stays the bright paper design.
3. **Map: small, at the bottom.** The locator is a small element (about 96 to 120 px, 80 on phones) in the bottom-right corner of the hero, with its one-line caption ("From the south, about 110 m up, looking north"). It changes with the slide. It may fold down to just the dot-map icon on phones. No big caption card. The caption line of each slide (title + AI label) sits bottom-left under the buttons or bottom-centre, small. The label "AI visualisation based on our 3D model, not a historical photograph" (zh: "AI 图像，依据我们的三维模型，并非历史照片") is always visible on every slide. Everything else about the locator (shared `<symbol id="gmap">`, dot placement from `site/assets/gallery/cameras.json`, EN and zh caption text, landmark verification) stays as in the earlier brief.
4. **Controls:** arrows at the vertical middle edges (subtle), dots and a pause button bottom-centre; autoplay about 8 s with a slow 1.2 s crossfade plus a very slow zoom (Ken Burns, 4 to 6 percent over the slide, disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`); swipe, keys, pause on hover/focus/hidden tab, no autoplay with reduced motion. A thin progress line for the current slide is welcome.
5. **The pictures are new 4K renders (5504 x 3072).** Candidates are already converted: `/opt/data/workspace/higgsfield_output/palace_life/hero4k/web/` with, per candidate `<scene>_4k_<take>`: `-3840.webp`, `-2560.webp`, `-1280.webp`, `-phone.webp` (1200x1800 centre crop for portrait phones, 2:3) and `-preview.jpg` (1600 px, for you to look at). Scenes: `aerial_ov_s_hills` (the whole garden from the south, golden hour, low hills on the left), `aerial_dashuifa` (the Great Fountain playing, summer afternoon), `aerial_jiuzhou` (Jiuzhou Qingyan, late-autumn dawn), `aerial_hanjing` (Hanjingtang in snow), `aerial_fuhai_isle` (Fuhai island hall, summer evening), `haiyan_dusk` (Haiyantang at dusk, eye level, lanterns; note its files are smaller). Two takes each (12 files).
   - **YOU choose.** Open every `-preview.jpg` with the Read tool and look at them yourself. For each scene pick the better take, and choose the 5 best scenes for the carousel (Tomasz wants it breathtaking, varied in season and light, nothing miniature-looking, no warped or bent mountains, no hard map edge, no modern object). Order: the brightest and most spectacular first, the dusk scene last or leave it out if it looks weak beside the others. Write your choices and one-line reasons into `/opt/data/cache/scratch/palace_r22_progress.md` and the final answer, and name the rejected ones.
   - Copy only the chosen files into `site/assets/gallery/` as `hero-<n>-<slug>-<size>.webp` (n = slide order). Slide 1: preload the 2560 (desktop) and the phone crop (`<link rel="preload" as="image" imagesrcset ... media>`) and use `<picture>` with `srcset` (1280, 2560, 3840) plus the phone crop at max-width 700 px, `sizes="100vw"`, `fetchpriority="high"`, real `width`/`height` (no layout shift) and a tiny blurred background colour (average colour of the picture) behind it while it decodes. Slides 2 to 5 come out of `<template>` after load, on idle, as in Küstrin; only the next slide is fetched ahead, never all five. 3840 is served only to wide, high-DPI screens (sizes/srcset handle that); do not make the first paint heavier than about 600 KB.
   - The earlier 2K files `hero-*-1600/640/phone.webp/.jpg` already in `site/assets/gallery/` (untracked) are superseded for the hero; delete them (`git status` shows them untracked) unless the lightbox needs one. The 10 `home-*.webp` gallery pictures stay for the gallery slots.
   - The lightbox "View large" link opens the 3840 webp.
6. **Captions:** as in the earlier brief (honest, EN and zh, "imagined" for anything the research does not support, the AI-invention line). Keep them short under the picture title. Name each building correctly from `research/buildings.csv`. The cameras in `cameras.json` for the new 4K takes are the same layout cameras as the 2K versions (same slug names `hero-dashuifa`, `hero-jiuzhou`, `hero-hanjing`, `hero-fuhai`, `hero-haiyan-dusk`); the overview scene `aerial_ov_s_hills` has camera: eye (150, 800, 1240), target (150, 3.6, -400), bearing 0 (looking north), fov 60, add it as `hero-overview` to `cameras.json`.
