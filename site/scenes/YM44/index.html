同乐园Tongle Yuan

See it in the 3D view

What it was

同乐园 Tongle Yuan, 'Garden of Shared Joy', is the court's theatre north of 曲院风荷 Quyuan Fenghe: two storeyed viewing buildings of five bays facing south, the three-tier stage 清音阁 Qingyin Ge facing north, 永日堂 Yongri Tang to the east, and through the middle the North-South Long Street 南北长街 with 抱朴草堂 Baopu Caotang on its west, leading north over twin bridges to 舍卫城 Shewei Cheng (S05 j82:27b-28a, E2146; E2148). It was reached from the Great Palace Gate by going east through the 东楼门 and about a li by boat (S202 j13, E2148). Under Qianlong banquets with plays were held here every year from the thirteenth of the first month for clansmen, Mongol princes, envoys and officials, and on imperial birthdays (E2148); S360 lists 155 dated items, 1738-1860 (E2152). Xianfeng held his birthday feast here in 1852 (S207, E1557). Malone describes a granite road between two rows of buildings, including the theatre, which 'looked like a business street in a city' (S02 p.94, E2067).

The leaf of 1744, its poem and preface

Not one of the Forty Scenes: it lies within the view of leaf 38, 坐石临流 Zuoshi Linliu (P0038). Qianlong's preface to that leaf (S05 j82:26a) ends '東為同樂園' after a pavilion over a rocky stream; the poem recalls the orchid-pavilion gathering of 永和. Malone captions the later edition of leaf 38: 'The theater is in the square courtyard at the right. Shops line both sides of the street which leads across a bridge to the gate of She Wei Ch'eng' (S02 p.95).

How it changed before 1860

The great stage 清音阁 of 同乐园 is given in metres on mhymy record 7297, converted from a Yangshi Lei drawing that the page does not name: lower central bay 5.50 m, side bays 4.51 m (front c. 14.5 m), platform 0.96 m, middle storey 14.53 m square inside the eaves, upper storey 13.09 m, eave columns 4.42 / 3.71 / 2.72 m, 23.94 m from the ground to the ridge; a plan of 道光十八年 (1838) to cut it to two storeys, 3.60 m lower, met objections from officials; whether it was carried out is not known here (S310 rec. 7297). The National Museum of China holds a card model (烫样) of the 同乐园 group; one report says it was made for Tongzhi's repair (S328, E2158), another gives no date (S922).

Inside

S202 j13 (E2148): the emperor and consorts in the five-bay main building, the guests in the east and west wings, each placed in an assigned room; a stage "about ten zhang deep and wide" (some 32 m, against about 14.5 m in the drawing's figures) in three tiers, gods let down from the top and demons up from below; plays with titles such as 清平见喜 and 万年甲子; actors chosen by the textile commissioners (织造), customs superintendents and salt offices until Daoguang sent the 南府 Nanfu players home in 1821 and eunuchs took over. Hanlin officials first sat in under Jiaqing (E2150). Attiret describes the court fair, which Malone places on this street (S02 p.95): eunuchs played the merchants, with quarters for silks and cloth and streets for porcelain and lacquer, and real goods from Peking dealers (S157, E2160).

What survives

No dig of 同乐园 has been found. The best figures for the lost buildings are those of the 清音阁 stage converted to metres on 梦回圆明园 Menghui Yuanmingyuan from an unnamed Yangshi Lei drawing (see above; S310, ysltd 7297). The shopping street ran straight for over 130 m north to 舍卫城 (S941).
