Light: the sun, the sky, the shadows, the haze and the night of Beautiful light are a choice for the look, not evidence: a clear day in the season you pick, by Beijing's clock. Clean is the historical view.

Night: no source places a lantern or says which rooms were lit before 1860. The lanterns along the galleries, before the gates and the main halls and at the landings, and the lit windows, are generated by fixed rules for the look of a festival night (assumed, C); the Assumed switch hides them.
